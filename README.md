
## Weakly Supervised ML for Rapeseed Feeding-Damage Scoring
</hr>
The problem:
The cabbage stem flea beetle (Psylliodes chrysocephala), in German: Großer Rapserdfloh,
is a major pest of oilseed rape (Brassica napus).
In the field it feeds on the young emerging cotyledons (=embryonic leaves found within a plant‘s
seed) and small true leaves in autumn (up to 5 leave stage).
Symptoms:
· Shot-Holing: Distinct, small, rounded circular holes on cotyledons and early true leaves.
· Pitting: Shallow bites and irregular indentations on young stems and developing leaves.
